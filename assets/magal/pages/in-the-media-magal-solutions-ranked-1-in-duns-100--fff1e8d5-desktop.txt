Magal Solutions Ranked #1 in Duns 100
Duns 100 Company Profile

Magal Solutions has been ranked #1 in the Homeland Security (HLS) category of the 2026 Dun’s 100 Defense Industry ranking.

Published by Dun & Bradstreet, the new ranking maps 60 companies across six areas of Israel’s defense industry, including defense integration, manufacturing, unmanned and autonomous systems, homeland security, intelligence and cyber, and tactical equipment.

The ranking considers a combination of quantitative and qualitative factors, including business strength, technological innovation, international activity, position within the defense value chain and operational relevance.

The launch of the ranking received coverage across leading Israeli business, technology and defense media outlets, all of which highlighted Magal’s #1 position in the Homeland Security category.
