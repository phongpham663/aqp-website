Fensor

Fensor is an advanced vibration sensor by Magal, incorporating accelerometer technology for intrusion detection. It meets military-grade standards and is endorsed by IMOD for its reliability. Fensor can be easily installed on different fence types, making it compatible with existing security setups. Its adaptive technology utilizes proprietary algorithms to adjust to weather conditions and minimize false alarms caused by vehicle movements or pedestrian activity. Fensor sets a new benchmark in perimeter security with optimized intrusion detection capabilities.
