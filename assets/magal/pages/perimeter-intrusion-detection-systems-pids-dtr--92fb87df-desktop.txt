DTR

DTR is a high-end Taut-Wire Perimeter Intrusion Detection System (PIDS) by Magal. Its design integrates the taut-wire fence as a sensor, making it a standalone solution. With exceptional performance in various weather conditions, including high winds and snowstorms, DTR offers a lifespan of over 30 years. It boasts a low False Alarm Rate (FAR) and high Probability of Detection (POD), resulting in reliable performance. DTR has proven its effectiveness in critical installations worldwide, enduring extreme environments and providing unmatched reliability.
