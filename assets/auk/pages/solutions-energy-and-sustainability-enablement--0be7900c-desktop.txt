THE PROBLEM

The challenge of understanding energy costs

Rising energy costs resemble a ‘black box’ expense with unclear ties to daily production, hindering improvement efforts.

Lack of detailed energy consumption breakdown by asset.

Delayed monthly analysis using utility bills.

Disconnect between energy-related costs and production SKUs (or PN).

THE SOLUTION

Energy data collection becomes effortless

Easily collect energy data from electrical switchboards to individual machines and link it with real-time production data through time series analysis.

Plug-and-play devices capture individual asset energy consumption.

Capture and visualize SKU-specific energy data for assets.
