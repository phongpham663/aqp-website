What you get from Auk

  • Data-driven bottleneck identification.

  • Bird’s-eye overview across asset/line/plant/business performance.

  • Closed-loop problem-solving.

The problem

Data challenges impacting decision-making

Manual, error-prone data collection hampers decision-making, causing performance disconnects and hindering front-line improvements.

  • Isolated, manual, delayed, and error-prone data collection.

  • Performance disconnect among organizational layers.

  • Difficulty turning front-line improvements into business results.

The Solution

Streamlining prioritization with real-time data

Real-time data across assets and organizational levels, using a single time metric, enables efficient comparison and prioritization of improvement opportunities, closely aligning front-line results with business metrics.

  • High-resolution data capturing

  • Closed-loop problem-solving cycle to validate business results against baseline.

  • Established performance connections from individual assets, production lines/cells, to plants and multi-plants.
