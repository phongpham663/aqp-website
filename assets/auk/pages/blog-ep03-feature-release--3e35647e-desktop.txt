Auk Community Note EP03: Feature Release

This week, we’re excited to announce the enhancement of 2 features, designed to deliver even greater control and visibility over your manufacturing operations.

What is New?

1. Issue Tagging on a block on existing line/group

Managing issues across a group of machines just got smarter. Our enhanced feature now allows users to tag and categorize issues (downtime reasons) directly at the group level. Whether it’s a recurring downtime event or a quality concern, you can pinpoint inefficiencies, assign priorities, and streamline resolution workflows—all from a single, intuitive interface.

2. Advanced Pareto Loss Duration Analysis

Understanding loss duration is critical to driving efficiency. We’ve upgraded the platform to display both weighted and summed Pareto loss hours for machine groups. This dual-view approach provides a comprehensive picture of where time and resources are slipping away, empowering you to prioritize high-impact improvements with precision.

Real Results, Real Value

Imagine a scenario where a manufacturing client uses these enhancements to identify a group of machines contributing to 20% of their downtime losses. By tagging the issue at the group level and analyzing weighted loss hours, they implement targeted interventions—reducing downtime by 12% in weeks!
