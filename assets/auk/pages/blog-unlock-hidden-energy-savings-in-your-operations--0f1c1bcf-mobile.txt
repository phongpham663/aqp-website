Auk Community Note EP02: Unlock Hidden Energy Savings in your Operations

In today’s competitive landscape, optimizing operational efficiency is more crucial than ever. One often overlooked opportunity lies in energy consumption — particularly the energy costs incurred when machines are idling.

Our client in the metal fabrication industry recently uncovered a 15% reduction in energy costs by simply addressing machine idling energy consumption — a clear win for both Operations Excellence and Sustainability.

Auk Industries’ IIoT solution empowers manufacturing companies to visualize energy usage in real time, down to the individual machines and specific product being produced. With clear, data-driven insights, you can:

👀 Identify energy drains by detecting machines consuming power during non-productive hours.

💰Quantify cost savings by translating energy data into dollar values.

🌲Drive sustainable operations by making informed decisions to reduce unnecessary consumption.
