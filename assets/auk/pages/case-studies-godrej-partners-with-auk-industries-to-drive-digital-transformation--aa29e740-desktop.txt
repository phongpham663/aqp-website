Godrej Partners with Auk Industries to Drive Digital Transformation!

From Data to Decisions: Godrej Boosts Productivity with Auk Industries

Godrej Consumer Products Indonesia (GCPI), part of the global FMCG leader Godrej, has embarked on a bold digital transformation journey — and the results are already visible.
Auk Industries’ Digital Operational Excellence solution was deployed across GCPI’s plants in Indonesia. In less than a month, the team gained:

✅ 100% real-time visibility of their shopfloor
✅ Ability to pinpoint production losses with data-driven precision
✅ A single source of truth for continuous improvement and productivity gains

Rima Nindisari, IT Business Partner – Finance & Non-Commercial Lead, GCPI

“At Godrej, we continuously look for ways to improve efficiency and empower our shopfloor teams. Partnering with Auk Industries has supported our digital transformation by providing reliable, real-time data that strengthens our improvement efforts.

Beyond their Industrial IoT system, Auk’s software features, ongoing development, and operational support have been valuable in helping us address bottlenecks and build team capability. This collaboration has contributed to measurable OEE improvements and equipped us with tools to drive sustainable manufacturing excellence.”
