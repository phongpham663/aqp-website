THE PROBLEM

Unaccounted raw material losses and hidden costs

In various industries, different UoMs for raw materials and finished goods result in unaccounted losses and hidden factory costs.

Mis-matched raw material input with production output.

Unaccounted material loss with no visibility on material conversion rate.

Hidden costs associated with material consumption.

THE SOLUTION

Real-time data capture simplifies accounting

Our advanced system captures real-time material consumption data and automatically converts Units of Measurement (UoM) for easy accounting.

Easy plug-and-play data capturing through asset I/O sensors.

Built-in UoM calculation per SKU per process.

Linked performance across individual assets, production lines/cells, to plants and multi-plants.
