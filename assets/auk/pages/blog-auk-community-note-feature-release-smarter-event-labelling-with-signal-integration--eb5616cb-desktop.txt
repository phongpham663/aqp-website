Auk Community Note: Feature Release – Smarter Event Labelling with Signal Integration

We’re excited to share a powerful enhancement to our Event Labelling feature that gives you greater visibility and control over what’s happening on your shop floor — with less manual work.

What’s New?

You can now connect manual button inputs or automatic sensor signals directly into our system to automatically detect and list events as they happen in real-time.

Whether it’s a:

🔄 Changeover
🛑 Breakdown
📦 Material shortage
⏸️ Idle time

These events will appear in a list, ready for your team to review and label with just a few clicks.

Why It Matters

This enhancement makes it easier to:

✅ Standardize event tracking across operators and shifts
🔍 Identify recurring issues faster with better data context
⏱️ Reduce time spent on manual entry
📈 Drive more accurate root cause analysis and CI actions

Let us know if you need any help implementing this great New Feature into your shop floor, making it Smarter and Better Operations everyday!
