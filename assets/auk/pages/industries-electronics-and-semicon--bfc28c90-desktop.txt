THE PROBLEM

Are you solving the right problems?

Driving high efficiency, meeting UPH and fill rate goals are challenging for factories that lack visibility and reliable production data

Manually collected data at the end of shift is low in resolution and unreliable. As a result, mapping loss reasons to the correct machines or lines, with the right time frame is almost impossible.

With ineffective root cause problem solving due to lack of real-time and reliable data, driving continuous improvement is an uphill task.

OUR SOLUTION

Run your factory with full transparency

With real-time, reliable data, you have full visibility on the problems that are impacting the factory performance immediately.

Having high resolution data means that all losses and bottlenecks can be clearly identified. Your resources can be spent to solve the right problem that moves the needle.

By eliminating time spent on manual data collection and problem identification, your team can spend most time solving problems that drive output and productivity gain.
