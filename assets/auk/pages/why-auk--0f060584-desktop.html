Trusted by the world’s leading consultants

How Auk transforms productivity

  • Deep data diving

    Our IoT edge processors capture 10,000 readings/second from connected sensors, tracing micro-stops and speed losses. Data is intelligently aggregated at the edge, providing all-time actual conditions.

  • Determining true baseline & gap

    Advanced algorithms analyse real-time streams with millions of data points, establishing product-specific standard cycle times and potential. Shop floor performance is continuously tracked.

  • Macro and micro analysis

    We analyse assets from both system and individual levels to identify issues. So as to derive top plant-level performance and solve the most critical problems.

The Auk difference makes it work

Why we succeedWhy others fail

Connect

Auk is designed for fast plug and play. Self-deployable within minutes across all machine types.

Most factories require costly infrastructure upgrades (e.g. outdated machines without PLC, PLC locked by OEM).

Build

Auk is a value stream-based multi-level system (machine, line, process group, plant, multi-plant level).

Most solutions are built at point level on isolated machines, making it difficult to identify top drivers for the line/plant.

Analyse

Auk analytics is built with deep lean and operations excellence expertise across industries. And codified from industry best practices.

Most tools in the market provide generic surface-level analytics designed with little understanding of manufacturing.

Value management

Auk is a closed-loop problem-solving performance management system. Designed to ensure seamless value capture.

Regular solutions are incomplete, relying on other systems or human intervention to be fully effective.
