Green Spot Co. Ltd goes live with Auk Industries — within weeks!

Thailand's leading soy milk maker Green Spot went live with Auk Industries within weeks – from zero to real-time production visibility.

As one of Thailand’s leading soy milk manufacturers, Green Spot is taking bold steps to embrace digital transformation to make real-time, data-driven decisions that boost productivity and achieve operational excellence!

We’re proud to partner with Green Spot on this journey 🛣️ !

Supadej Chirasavinuprapand, General Manager, Green Spot

“Thank you for being such a strong partner on this journey. At Green Spot, we believe that embracing digital transformation is key to achieving operational excellence and staying ahead in today’s fast-changing landscape. With your innovative solutions and support, we’re excited to harness the power of real-time, data-driven insights to boost productivity and create even greater value for our customers.”
