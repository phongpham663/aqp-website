About Event Security

At your Christmas market, festival or other event, we provide a mix of cameras in both public and private areas. Thanks to the local network we set up for our cameras, you can easily monitor the situation in real time.

How it Works
To properly secure your event, we make sure to sit down with you in a timely manner to discuss what risks exist and how you can minimize them through a good security plan. At your Christmas market, festival or other event, we provide a mix of eye-catching camera surveillance and manpower, as well as, for example, the security of temporary spaces where your valuables are stored.

Our cameras are managed remotely by our private alarm center, who can take quick action when an incident occurs. That way, you don't have to worry about calling in the police, for example, when necessary.

Our solutions

Watchtower 360

Watchtower 360 monitors your location in all directions with five cameras and a compact frame for 360-degree coverage. Easy to transport and set up, even in confined spaces.

Watchtower AI

Prevent false alarms with our innovative AI module: instant, high-quality analysis of the entire footage on-site.

Watchtower

This mobile camera mast is the solution to which we owe our name, and rightfully so. Reliable and affordable, this high-tech solution to secure your location has it all.

Watchtower Celsius

The Watchtower Celsius continuously monitors the temperature at your site and alerts your team in the event of abnormal heat build-up, rapid temperature rises, or smoke or fire.

Watchtower Solar

Our Watchtower Thermal: impenetrable security for expansive areas and large perimeters. It increases the surveillance area up to 254,800 ft². With two cameras on each side, the total range extends to as much as 1,000 ft.

Watchtower Box

The Watchtower Box is our most flexible and versatile solution, designed for environments with limited spaces, both indoor and outdoor. It offers a variety of mounting options, including scaffolding, containers, lighting posts or downpipes.

Watchtower Alarmkit

Locked rooms are easily overlooked in securing a location. With the Watchtower Alarmkit, you can secure these areas quickly, cost-effectively and wirelessly. Ideal for emergencies or temporary projects.

Watchtower Thermal

Our Watchtower Thermal: impenetrable security for expansive areas and large perimeters. It increases the surveillance area up to 254,800 ft². With two cameras on each side, the total range extends to as much as 1,000 ft.

Watchtower Security Plan

We always meet in person and examine your location or object to be secured. We make a risk analysis and a customized security plan. This way security is the least of your worries.
