Why you need an NVR?

Upgrading your existing cameras with an NVR

Sometimes, upgrading your existing cameras with a Network Video Recorder (NVR) is essential to harness the power of AI-enabled data analysis. NVRs provide the processing power and storage capabilities required for advanced AI algorithms to analyze video data in real-time, enabling features such as object recognition and behavior analysis. By integrating NVRs with existing cameras, businesses can unlock valuable insights, improve security, and optimize operations without having to replace their entire camera infrastructure.

      Frequently asked questions

      Where is OVOPARK based?

      Our company is headquartered in China, with a dedicated overseas business department to cater to our global clients. You can easily reach out to us through our website, email or WhatsApp.

      How can I check the data with your analytics software/Apps?

      With our analytics software, you can view live video and access past recordings at any time. Our technology provides real-time and periodic people counting reports on the condition of your store. Simply ensure that the devices are connected to the system. For more information, feel free to reach out to our sales team.

      Can I view periodic Excel reports with data comparisons across stores?

      Yes, generating multi-store comparison reports is easy. Simply select the time range and data conditions you need, and you can easily export the results to Excel.

      Can I connect my hardware equipment to your platform?

      The OVOPARK Analytics platform boasts strong compatibility and can seamlessly integrate with mainstream devices on the market. To ensure a smooth connection, please provide photos of your existing equipment and related product information. The professional technical team at OVOPARK will then furnish you with a perfect integration scheme.

      Can the people counting system omit the trolley count?

      Yes, our people counting system is capable of differentiating and omitting the trolley count due to the trolley's height being below 1.3 meters.
