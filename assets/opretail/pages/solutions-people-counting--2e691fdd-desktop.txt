People counting

The people counting solution sets a new standard for understanding and leveraging footfall data, elevating the potential for optimizing customer experiences and overall business performance.

Solution details
Footfall counting

By analyzing footfall trend patterns on a daily, monthly, and yearly basis, businesses can gain valuable insights into customer traffic and behavior.

Passersby counting

Gain a deeper understanding of the foot traffic in the vicinity of the store, identify peak hours and days, understand seasonal variations, and assess the impact of marketing efforts on customer engagement.

Count group as one entry

Accurately track foot traffic while differentiating between individual visitors and groups, providing a comprehensive understanding of customer behavior.
