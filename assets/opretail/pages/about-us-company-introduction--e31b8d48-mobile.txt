About us

OVOPARK Technology Co., Ltd. is a pioneering AI-driven retail technology innovator transforming retail operations through intelligent video analytics and cloud-based SaaS solutions. Our platform delivers comprehensive retail intelligence with precision analytics for customer footfall, dwell time & store heatmap, store performance management, shelf optimization, AI-powered staff training, and advanced loss prevention.

We empower retailers worldwide with actionable insights to enhance customer experiences, optimize operational efficiency, and drive measurable revenue growth. With a proven global footprint serving 4,000 leading brands and 300,000+ retail locations across 50+ countries, OVOPARK is the trusted partner for data-driven retail transformation in the digital era.

The R&D Team

Currently, OVOPARK boasts a workforce of almost 400 employees, with 60% dedicated to R&D. These individuals specialize in video-related fields such as the video analysis, embedded intelligent products, and AI. Notably, our AI Research Institute is headed by a team member with a doctorate in AI and Image Algorithm.
