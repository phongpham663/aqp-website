Why you need an NVR?
Upgrading your existing cameras with an NVR

Sometimes, upgrading your existing cameras with a Network Video Recorder (NVR) is essential to harness the power of AI-enabled data analysis. NVRs provide the processing power and storage capabilities required for advanced AI algorithms to analyze video data in real-time, enabling features such as object recognition and behavior analysis. By integrating NVRs with existing cameras, businesses can unlock valuable insights, improve security, and optimize operations without having to replace their entire camera infrastructure.
