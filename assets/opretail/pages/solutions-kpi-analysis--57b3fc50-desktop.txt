Store KPI analysis

Our store KPI analysis solution empowers businesses to gain deep insights into store's performance and drive informed decision-making for sustainable growth.

Solution details
Walk-in rate analysis

By tallying the foot traffic entering your store and calculating the walk-in rate, managers gain a valuable metric for evaluating the store's appeal.

Conversion rate analysis

By tracking foot traffic and connecting POS data to capture sales figures, businesses can calculate the conversion rate (purchasers divided by walk-in count), enabling managers to gain deeper insights into the store's operational effectiveness and customer conversion potential.

Multi-location ranking

Leverage the power of our SaaS solution to compare walk-in rates and conversion rates across multiple chain stores, providing a comprehensive store ranking chart.
